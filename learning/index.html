Core Principle

There is no universally “best” campaign structure.

The correct structure depends on how the customer buys and how much reliable signal the account can generate.

01

Budget

Can each campaign/ad set collect enough data?
02

Signal Volume

How many meaningful conversions happen each week?
03

Buyer Intent

Are the customer needs truly different?
04

Sales Cycle

Does the prospect need education before converting?
05

Creative Diversity

Can creative do the segmentation instead of audiences?
Senior media-buyer question: “How much data do I have, how different are the buyer intentions, and how much education does the customer need before converting?”

Case Study 01 · C3 Studios

B2B video editing needed a full-funnel education path.

C3 Studios sources, vets, and manages permanent video editing teams. The offer solves an operational problem for agencies and businesses producing video at scale.

Owner: Robert DeCorah
Location: Cedar Rapids, Iowa
Coverage: March–August 2025
Primary objective: B2B lead generation
Primary audience: agencies and content-heavy businesses
Business Problem

“You film, we handle the rest.”

Prospects were not simply buying editing hours. They were evaluating whether to replace freelancer management with a managed editing team, scalable tiers, and a 30-day replacement guarantee.

Why funnel stages made sense: the buyer had to first identify the editing bottleneck, then understand the managed-team solution, then become ready for a sales conversation.
C3 Studios · Strategy04
C3 Studios · Funnel Design

Problem awareness → solution education → qualified inquiry.

TOFU

Editing bottleneck

“Your agency doesn't have a content problem. It has an editing bottleneck.”

Focus: freelancer chaos, capacity, founder time, production delays.
MOFU

Managed-team model

Explain sourcing, vetting, scalable capacity, team management, and replacement guarantee.

Focus: mechanism and risk reduction.
BOFU

Book / qualify

“Tell us your monthly video volume and we'll show you the right editing setup.”

Focus: company type, volume, timeline, editors needed.

Case Study 05 · KLN Consulting

Restricted targeting made creative and qualification more important.

Training case for a financial consulting business serving Delaware and Arizona.

Owner: Cyerra Danielle White
Credit consulting / repair education
Business funding readiness
Tax consulting
Primary channel: Meta Lead Generation
Structure Rule

Consolidate inside the service line.

Do not automatically mix credit consulting, business funding, and tax consulting into one undifferentiated campaign if the buying intentions and follow-up processes are meaningfully different.

Campaign 1

Credit Consulting

1 broad/compliant ad set · multiple educational/problem angles.
Campaign 2

Business Funding

1 broad/compliant ad set · funding-readiness creative angles.
Campaign 3

Tax Consulting

1 broad/compliant ad set · planning/preparation angles.

Knowledge Check

Can you defend the structure decision?

01A local business has $40/day, one city, one lead objective, and 12 ad sets. What problem would you investigate first?
02A B2B service has a long sales cycle and prospects do not understand the offer. Why might TOFU/MOFU/BOFU still make sense?
03A campaign's CPL increased from $70 to $100, but qualified-lead rate doubled. Did performance necessarily get worse?
04When can creative replace excessive audience segmentation?
05Why should KLN separate materially different service lines even while using a consolidated philosophy?
Goal: explain the business reason behind the account structure in plain English. If you cannot explain why the structure exists, you probably should not build it yet.

Final Takeaways

Think like a media buyer, not a template operator.

01

Match structure to signal.

Do not fragment a small budget into more learning environments than the account can support.

02

Let creative carry meaning.

Different pain points can often live inside one larger audience when the conversion objective is shared.

03

Optimize deeper than CPL.

Qualified leads, booked calls, accepted opportunities, CAC, and revenue are stronger business signals.

“How much data do I have, how different are the buyer intentions, and how much education does the customer need before converting?”
