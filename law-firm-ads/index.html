Cross-Platform Acquisition Model

Two platforms. Different jobs. One lead-quality system.

Google Ads
Capture existing legal intent.
High-intent accident and injury queries.
Tight geographic control and service-area relevance.
Exact and phrase match where CPCs are expensive.
Search-term governance, negatives, and landing-page alignment.
Optimize beyond form fills when qualified intake or signed-case feedback is available.
Meta Ads
Create and capture demand through creative.
One lead campaign with a broad or policy-compliant audience.
One primary ad set to protect signal density.
Multiple creatives representing accident types and pain points.
Qualification questions before the lead reaches intake.
Creative becomes a major segmentation layer instead of excessive ad-set fragmentation.

Atty. Virginia King · ViKing LawPersonal Injury / Motor Vehicle Accident Lead Generation
Case Study 01 · Client Snapshot

Build around the moment someone realizes the accident has become a legal problem.

The acquisition strategy centered on car and motor-vehicle injury demand, with Google capturing people already searching for legal representation while Meta addressed the pain points that often precede an attorney search.

Campaign Challenge
PI search traffic is expensive and highly competitive.
Raw lead volume alone does not indicate viable case quality.
Prospects can enter from different moments: injury, insurance pressure, fault disputes, or unresolved treatment concerns.
Lead forms needed to filter accident relevance before intake follow-up.
ViKing Law03
ViKing Law · Google + Meta Execution

Search intent on Google. Pain-point intent on Meta.

Google Ads Structure
Car accident and injury-lawyer search themes.
Phrase and exact match for stronger query control.
Negative keyword expansion to remove research, jobs, unrelated legal services, and weak-intent traffic.
Dedicated landing-page alignment by accident/service theme where volume supports it.
Meta Andromeda-Style Structure
1 lead-generation campaign.
1 primary consolidated ad set.
Multiple static creative angles instead of many small audiences.
Pain points: insurance adjuster pressure, rear-end crashes, medical treatment, fault disputes, settlement questions.
The account structure separated platform roles while keeping one common business objective: qualified PI intake, not the cheapest possible lead.

Peters Law Firm · Google + Meta Execution

Control expensive search traffic while letting creative diversity do more of the work on Meta.

Google: Accident IntentCar accident lawyer, injury attorney, truck accident and closely related bottom-funnel themes.
Google: Query GovernanceSearch-term reviews, negative expansion, geo control, bid/budget concentration on stronger intent.
Google: Conversion QualityLead form or call is only the first step; qualification and signed-case feedback become more important as volume grows.
Meta: AuthorityAttorney-led messaging and clear explanation of what an injured person can expect next.

Case Study 03 · Client Snapshot

In an oversaturated PI market, Google cannot be the only growth engine.

New Orleans PI search is highly competitive. The strategy keeps Google focused on high-intent demand capture while Meta is structured to find accident victims through multiple pain-point and accident-type creatives.

Core Challenge

Car, truck, commercial-vehicle, and 18-wheeler cases require different messaging.

Measurement Framework

The KPI is not the form submission. The KPI gets better as the lead moves downstream.

Stage 01
Click / VisitSearch or Meta traffic enters the legal funnel.
Stage 02
Lead / CallProspect submits an inquiry or initiates contact.
Stage 03
Qualified IntakeAccident, injury, geography, timing, and representation status are validated.
Stage 04
ConsultationThe lead reaches the legal intake or attorney-review stage.
Stage 05
Signed CaseThe strongest downstream signal for media optimization and budget allocation.
A $200 lead that becomes a viable case can be more valuable than a $40 lead that intake rejects. PI media buying becomes stronger when budget follows downstream quality, not just front-end CPL.
