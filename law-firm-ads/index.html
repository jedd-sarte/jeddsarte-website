Portfolio Takeaway

Google captures the person already searching. Meta gives the firm more ways to find the right accident victim before the search.

For PI, the strongest paid-media system uses Search for explicit legal demand and a consolidated Meta structure for creative-led incremental acquisition.
Operating Principles
Control expensive queries aggressively.
Consolidate Meta learning where conversion goals are shared.
Diversify by pain point and accident scenario through creative.
Qualify leads before treating CPL as success.
Feed downstream case quality back into budget decisions.
