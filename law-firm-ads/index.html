The Major Law Firm · KPIs10
Portfolio KPI Pattern · Google Ads

CTR tells you relevance. CPL tells you efficiency. Lost IS tells you what is limiting scale.

CTR

7.8% to 10.6%

Useful relevance diagnostic, but not a business outcome by itself.

Average CPC

$76 to $135

PI economics make weak clicks very expensive.

Google CPL

$525 to $1,050

The operating threshold changes by market competitiveness.

Lost IS

13% to 29%

Budget and rank reveal why additional high-intent demand is being missed.

A high CPL does not automatically mean a bad campaign. In PI, the media buyer has to interpret CPL alongside CPC, conversion rate, impression-share loss, and downstream case quality.

Meta Ads KPI Pattern12
Measurement Framework

The KPI gets more valuable as the prospect moves deeper into intake.

Stage 01
Click / VisitCTR and CPC describe traffic economics.
Stage 02
Lead / CallCPL becomes the first major acquisition cost.
Stage 03
Qualified IntakeQualified rate and cost per qualified lead improve the signal.
Stage 04
ConsultationThe legal team validates fit and case potential.
Stage 05
Signed CaseThe strongest downstream signal for budget allocation.
A $200 lead that becomes a viable case can be more valuable than a $40 lead that intake rejects. PI media buying improves when budget follows downstream quality, not simply the cheapest CPL.
