Strategy

Build a technically sound international SEO framework

  • Create unique localized content instead of duplicating global copy market to market.
  • Strengthen canonical tags, regional sitemaps, and site-speed fundamentals.
  • Track regional traffic, rankings, and conversions to support ongoing optimization.
  • Maintain brand consistency while differentiating regional search intent.
The objective was not simply more content. It was clearer regional ownership and stronger technical signals.
