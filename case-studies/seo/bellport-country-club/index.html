The challenge

A busy-season business entering with declining sales and lead volume

The business entered the engagement with a documented 10% drop in sales and 6% drop in leads, while the website had serious speed issues, oversized images, and limited technical SEO investment.

Performance problems

Oversized images and slow page loads created technical barriers to both user experience and organic performance.

Underdeveloped SEO program

The site needed a structured roadmap connecting technical fixes, valuable content, internal linking, and commercial search demand.

Strategy

Fix the technical foundation, then build around valuable search demand

  • Improve page speed and technical SEO until mobile pages passed Google's Core Web Vitals.
  • Optimize site imagery to reduce load time.
  • Build a six-month data-driven SEO roadmap around economically valuable search topics.
  • Overhaul existing content and create new content where demand justified it.
  • Optimize content for multiple positions and features within Google SERPs.
  • Implement a strategic internal-linking structure.
The growth plan connected technical health, content quality, and internal authority instead of treating them as separate SEO tasks.
