The challenge

Organic growth in an extremely competitive legal category

Personal injury keywords are highly competitive, so winning visibility required a coordinated plan across content, site architecture, local SEO, on-page optimization, and technical SEO.

High-value search competition

The market required a deliberate strategy around economically valuable queries rather than generic traffic growth.

Need to reduce paid dependency

Organic growth was intended to lessen reliance on Google Ads and Local Services Ads over time.

Strategy

Rebuild the SEO foundation around intent and local visibility

  • Redesign the website to improve usability, conversions, and search-friendly site architecture.
  • Build a content roadmap based on search volume, economic value, and transactional, informational, and commercial intent.
  • Write and optimize content to earn visibility across multiple SERP placements.
  • Optimize Google Business Profile and local citations to improve Local SEO and Google Maps exposure.
  • Strengthen technical SEO and on-page SEO across the site.
The strategy treated SEO as an acquisition system, not a publishing calendar.

High-value organic visibility with sustained keyword growth
