What I can help implement or audit

The exact setup depends on the business model, but the goal is consistent: establish a trustworthy conversion signal before asking automated bidding to make budget decisions.

01 • GOOGLE ADS

Conversion actions

02 • GTM

06 • QA

Validation and testing

When It Matters Most

Fix measurement before scaling spend

More budget does not solve bad data. If the conversion action is weak, automated bidding can simply get better at finding more of the wrong outcome.

  • Accounts moving from Maximize Clicks into conversion-based bidding
  • Lead generation businesses with major quality differences between leads
  • Wix, WordPress, Shopify, or cross-domain purchase journeys
  • Accounts where GA4 and Google Ads totals do not line up
  • Businesses importing CRM or offline outcomes
  • Campaigns using tCPA, tROAS, or value-based bidding

FAQ

Conversion tracking questions

What conversion tracking can you help with?

Google Ads conversions, GA4 events, GTM, enhanced conversions, offline conversion tracking, lead-quality feedback loops, and cross-domain tracking where the stack supports it.

Why does this matter before changing bids?

Smart bidding depends on the signal it receives. If a low-value action is treated like a primary conversion, the bidding system can optimize aggressively toward that action instead of revenue or qualified opportunities.
