JeddSarte Paid Media MCP

A private paid media management integration developed by Jedd Sarte for authorized advertising accounts.

About the App

JeddSarte Paid Media MCP supports Google Ads campaign reporting, account auditing, campaign creation and management, conversion configuration, keyword planning, and related advertising account-management workflows for accounts the authorized operator is permitted to manage.
