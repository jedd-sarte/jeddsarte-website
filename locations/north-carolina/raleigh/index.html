Raleigh paid search strategy

Separate commercial evaluation from informational search behavior.

Raleigh and the broader Triangle have strong demand across technology, B2B, healthcare, professional services, real estate, and local services. Higher-consideration categories can attract large amounts of educational traffic. Paid search should identify which queries, services, locations, and conversion paths represent real buying intent.

01 · B2B Intent

Distinguish research from commercial evaluation

Search terms, negatives, match types, messaging, and landing pages help separate prospects evaluating a provider from users simply looking for information.

02 · Pipeline Signals

Measure progression beyond the first conversion

Qualified consultations, appointments, opportunities, CRM stages, purchases, and revenue can provide stronger optimization feedback than raw lead counts.

03 · Service Geography

Make local targeting reflect customer economics

Raleigh and surrounding service areas can differ in customer density, lead quality, serviceability, and value. Geographic reporting should make those differences visible.
