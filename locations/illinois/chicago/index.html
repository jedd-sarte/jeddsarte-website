Chicago paid search strategy

Control expensive demand before trying to scale it.

Chicago is a dense, competitive market across legal, B2B, healthcare, contractors, real estate, and e-commerce. Broad targeting and weak conversion definitions can become expensive quickly. The account should identify which queries, services, territories, landing pages, and customer segments deserve additional budget.

01 · High-CPC Control

Reduce the cost of irrelevant demand

Search-term reviews, negatives, match types, ad relevance, bidding inputs, and landing-page alignment help keep spend concentrated around commercially useful searches.

02 · Metro Economics

Let geography follow business value

Chicago and surrounding service areas can perform differently. Geographic reporting and structure should reveal where lead quality, customer value, or service economics change.

03 · Qualified Outcomes

Give stronger conversions more weight

Qualified calls, appointments, opportunities, purchases, signed clients, and revenue provide stronger optimization feedback than treating every form submission equally.
