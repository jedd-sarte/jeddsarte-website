New York PPC strategy for competitive search markets

High cost per click does not automatically make a market unprofitable. The bigger question is whether campaigns can distinguish valuable demand from research traffic, weak-fit inquiries, and searches outside the advertiser's profitable service area. That requires disciplined search-term management, useful negative keywords, landing-page alignment, and conversion signals that represent meaningful outcomes.

For longer-cycle B2B and professional-services acquisition, I prefer qualified opportunities and downstream pipeline feedback when the stack supports it. For local lead generation, booked appointments, qualified calls, signed clients, or other business outcomes can be more useful than counting every form submission equally.
