How I approach DFW Google Ads management

I start by identifying the products, services, and customer types that matter most financially. From there, I build or refine campaign structure so geography, intent, and budget are easier to control. That helps reduce wasted spend and makes it easier to see where growth is actually coming from.

For home-service and contractor accounts, the key may be job type, service radius, booked estimates, and close rate. For B2B and professional services, qualified opportunities and pipeline value matter more. For e-commerce, the focus shifts toward purchase value, margin, product demand, and query economics.

Tracking should reflect the business model. Calls, forms, booked jobs, CRM stages, purchases, and offline conversions can all be useful if they are configured cleanly and tied back to the correct campaigns.
