Texas PPC strategy by market and customer value

For local-service businesses, geography and job value can matter as much as keyword selection. For B2B and professional services, the bigger challenge is often separating decision-stage demand from broad research traffic. E-commerce and multi-location businesses may need a different structure again, with revenue, margin, or store-level performance guiding budget decisions.

I focus on campaign structure, search-term quality, negative keywords, landing-page relevance, conversion tracking, and the downstream outcomes that matter most. Where available, qualified leads, booked jobs, opportunities, purchases, and revenue can provide better optimization signals than raw form submissions or clicks.
