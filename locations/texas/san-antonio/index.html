San Antonio PPC strategy built around local economics

Paid search can waste budget when campaigns treat every area, service, and lead the same. Stronger performance usually comes from separating geography, search intent, service lines, and customer value so spend follows the combinations most likely to create revenue.

Search-term governance

Lead-quality measurement

Calls and forms should be measured by what happens after the conversion. Qualified leads, booked appointments, estimates, opportunities, purchases, and revenue provide stronger optimization signals than raw volume.
