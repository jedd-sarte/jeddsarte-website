Louisiana PPC strategy built around qualified demand

For local lead generation, I focus on the searches that signal a person is actually looking for a provider, attorney, contractor, clinic, or other service. That means tighter keyword intent, negative keyword coverage, location targeting, landing-page relevance, and conversion actions that represent meaningful inquiries instead of every click or page interaction.

For businesses serving multiple parishes or metro areas, budget should follow opportunity. A service area with stronger close rates or higher customer value may deserve a different bidding approach than a lower-value area. Where the data is available, qualified leads, booked consultations, jobs, purchases, and revenue should guide optimization more than raw lead volume.
