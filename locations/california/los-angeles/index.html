How I approach Los Angeles Google Ads

I start with the business model and unit economics. Which services or products have the strongest margin? Which customer types are most valuable? Which geographic areas can the business serve profitably? Those answers help shape campaign structure and determine where budget should be concentrated.

In a sprawling market like Los Angeles, geographic reporting deserves close attention. Performance can vary materially across neighborhoods, cities, and service areas. When those differences are visible, budgets and bids can be adjusted based on actual lead quality and revenue potential instead of broad metro averages.

For lead-generation accounts, I also look beyond basic form fills. Qualified calls, booked consultations, appointments, sales opportunities, and offline outcomes can create a stronger feedback loop for optimization. For e-commerce, the focus shifts toward purchase value, margin, product-level demand, and query economics.
