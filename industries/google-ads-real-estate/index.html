Acquisition Strategy

Structure paid search around the transaction you want

Agents, brokers, property managers, developers, and real estate businesses need different campaign structures. The account should make it clear which locations, property types, and search themes produce qualified opportunities.

01 • INTENT

Buyer and seller separation

Separate distinct search journeys when messaging, economics, and landing pages are meaningfully different.

02 • LOCAL

Market-level control

Use location targeting, exclusions, and budget allocation based on the actual markets and properties the business serves.

03 • QUALIFICATION

Lead quality

Forms and calls should capture enough information to distinguish serious inquiries from low-value or irrelevant demand.

04 • TRACKING

Downstream outcomes

Where possible, connect campaign data with qualified appointments, applications, leases, or closed deals instead of optimizing only to initial leads.

05 • LANDING

Message match

Send high-intent searches to pages that match the property, market, service, or offer being advertised.

06 • BUDGET

Opportunity allocation

Shift spend toward the markets, listings, services, or search themes that are producing stronger commercial outcomes.
