B2B Paid Search Management

What ongoing B2B PPC management includes

The work goes beyond launching campaigns. I manage the search-to-pipeline path so targeting, conversion signals, and optimization decisions stay connected to qualified business opportunities.

01 • CONTROL

Search demand and account structure

Campaign structure, keywords, match types, search terms, negatives, budgets, ads, and landing-page alignment are reviewed around the services and buyers that matter most.

02 • MEASURE

Conversion and lead-quality signals

Primary conversions are separated from weaker actions, and downstream qualified-lead or opportunity feedback is incorporated when the stack supports it.

03 • OPTIMIZE

Ongoing decisions and reporting

Performance reviews focus on CPA, conversion quality, search demand, budget allocation, and the next actions most likely to improve useful pipeline.
