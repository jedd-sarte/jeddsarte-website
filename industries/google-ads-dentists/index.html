Patient Acquisition

Build campaigns around treatments, location, and intent

A dental practice should be able to see which searches and services are driving the strongest opportunities, whether the focus is implants, emergency dentistry, cosmetic services, general dentistry, or another priority treatment.

01 • TREATMENTS

Service-line structure

Separate high-value procedures when their economics, urgency, keywords, or landing-page needs are meaningfully different.

02 • LOCAL

Geographic control

Use location settings and exclusions that reflect the practice's real catchment area instead of paying for unreachable patients.

03 • INTENT

Search-term quality

Prioritize people actively looking for a provider and use negatives to reduce research, jobs, training, and irrelevant queries.

04 • TRACKING

Appointment signals

Track calls, forms, booking actions, and downstream quality where available so optimization is not based on empty micro-conversions.

05 • LANDING

Treatment relevance

Match high-intent searches to pages that clearly explain the procedure, practice credibility, location, and next step.

06 • VALUE

Patient economics

Budget and bidding decisions should reflect the value and close rate of different treatments, not just raw lead totals.
