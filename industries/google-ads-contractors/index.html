Local Search Strategy

Control spend around the services and areas that matter

The account should make it easy to see which services, neighborhoods, search terms, and landing pages are creating real sales opportunities.

01 • SERVICES

Service-line structure

Separate high-value services when their keywords, margins, urgency, or landing-page needs are meaningfully different.

02 • LOCAL

Geographic control

Use location settings, exclusions, radius logic, and budget allocation that reflect where the business actually serves customers.

03 • INTENT

Keyword discipline

Prioritize searches with real hiring intent and use search-term reviews and negatives to reduce research, DIY, job-seeker, or unrelated traffic.

04 • CONVERSIONS

Lead quality tracking

Track the calls, forms, estimate requests, purchases, or other actions that represent genuine demand, not every possible micro-event.

05 • LANDING PAGES

Service message match

Match high-intent search traffic to pages that clearly explain the relevant service, market, proof, and next action.

06 • BUDGET

Seasonal allocation

Shift spend as demand, capacity, weather, margins, and priority services change instead of running the same allocation all year.

FAQ

Google Ads for contractors

How should contractor campaigns be structured?

Structure should follow the business. Services with different economics, intent, or locations usually deserve separate control, while unnecessary fragmentation should be avoided.

What should count as a primary conversion?

Use actions closely tied to revenue or real sales opportunities, such as qualified calls, estimate requests, forms, or purchases. Low-value interactions can remain secondary diagnostics.
