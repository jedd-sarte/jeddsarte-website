Patient Acquisition

Healthcare PPC needs more than click volume

The account should make it easier to understand which searches, services, locations, and landing pages produce valuable patient opportunities. That requires clean tracking and enough structure to control budget without over-fragmenting campaigns.

01 • SERVICES

Service-line structure

Separate priority treatments or specialties when their search behavior, patient value, or landing-page needs are materially different.

02 • LOCAL

Geographic intent

Target the communities the practice can realistically serve and exclude areas that create low-value spend.

03 • SEARCH

Commercial queries

Prioritize searches showing real provider or treatment intent and use negatives to reduce research, jobs, and irrelevant traffic.

04 • TRACKING

Appointment signals

Measure calls, forms, booking actions, and downstream quality where available so bidding is not trained on weak signals.

05 • LANDING

Service relevance

Match each high-intent search with a page that explains the service, credibility, location, and next step clearly.

06 • VALUE

Patient economics

Use treatment value and close rate to guide budget decisions rather than treating every lead as equally valuable.
