C3 StudiosCedar Rapids, Iowa · March to August 2025 · Meta Lead Generation
Case Study 01 · Client Snapshot & Challenge

Turning an operational pain point into agency client acquisition.

C3 Studios sources, vets, and manages permanent video editors for agencies and content teams. The offer removes recruiting, replacement, and day-to-day editor management from the client.

Business Challenge

The buyer needed education before the sales call.

Agency owners already understood editing, but not necessarily C3's managed-team model.
Cold prospects had to see the cost of fragmented freelancer management.
The offer required trust around quality, scalability, and the 30-day replacement guarantee.
Primary conversion goal: qualified lead and booked sales conversation.
C3 Studios02
C3 Studios · Strategy & Execution

A sequential funnel moved agencies from problem recognition to sales intent.

TOFU
Problem AwarenessEditing bottlenecks, unreliable freelancers, missed deadlines, and the management burden of building an internal editing function.
MOFU
Solution EducationManaged editor model, sourcing and vetting process, scalable capacity, workflow control, and replacement guarantee.
BOFU
Lead GenerationDirect response for warm prospects with qualification around agency type, monthly volume, editor requirements, and timing.
Editing Bottleneck“Your agency doesn't have a content problem. It has an editing bottleneck.”
Freelancer ManagementReduce the time spent sourcing, replacing, and coordinating individual editors.
Scale Without HiringIncrease production capacity without building another internal department.

USA Fitness Equipment DepotPasadena, Texas · Meta Lead Generation
Case Study 02 · Client Snapshot & Challenge

One commercial market, multiple purchase motivations.

The campaign targeted gym owners, entrepreneurs opening facilities, and recreational or commercial facilities purchasing strength and cardio equipment.

Business Challenge
A startup gym owner behaves differently from an established facility upgrading equipment.
Some buyers care about refurbished value, others about premium new inventory.
Facility planning can be the entry point even when the eventual sale is equipment.
The account needed creative diversity without fragmenting the signal into many tiny audiences.

Daryl Gray Injury LawyersNew Orleans, Louisiana · Car, Truck & 18-Wheeler Accident Lead Generation
Case Study 04 · Client Snapshot & Challenge

Building another acquisition path in an oversaturated PI market.

Google Search remains valuable for explicit legal demand, but PI auctions can be extremely expensive. Meta created another channel for reaching accident victims through highly specific problem and accident scenarios.

Business Challenge
Car and truck accident leads can vary dramatically in legal value.
Raw CPL can hide poor-fit or non-accident inquiries.
Creative needed to speak to multiple accident and insurance pain points.
Lead forms had to qualify intent before the attorney review stage.

KLN ConsultingDelaware & Arizona · Meta Lead Generation
Case Study 05 · Client Snapshot & Challenge

Generating financial-service leads under platform targeting constraints.

KLN Consulting provides credit consulting, business funding guidance, credit-repair support, and tax consulting. Many of these services require more careful platform compliance than ordinary local-service advertising.

Business Challenge
Audience targeting could not depend on highly sensitive personal attributes.
Copy had to avoid implying knowledge of a user's personal financial condition.
Credit, funding, and tax services represent materially different buyer intentions.
The account needed consolidation without mixing unrelated conversion paths.
KLN Consulting14
KLN Consulting · Strategy & Execution

Consolidation happened inside each service line.

Campaign 1

Credit Consulting

Broad compliant delivery with creative around education, credit-profile understanding, and next-step planning.

Campaign 2

Business Funding

Funding-readiness messaging focused on preparation and business financial positioning before application.

Campaign 3

Tax Consulting

Planning and preparation angles kept separate from the credit and funding sales conversation.

EducationUnderstand the current situation and identify the next practical steps.
PreparationBuild readiness before applying for funding or entering a major financial decision.
ConsultationConvert broad compliant traffic through stronger lead qualification and service-specific follow-up.

Portfolio Takeaway

Five businesses. Five different reasons for the structure used.

C3 Studios

Sequential Full Funnel

Longer B2B consideration cycle required education before direct response.

USA Fitness

Creative-Led Consolidation

One commercial objective supported multiple buyer motivations inside a broad delivery pool.

Zahlco

Signal Consolidation

A small local budget could not efficiently support thirteen ad sets.

Daryl Gray

Quality-Led PI Acquisition

Multiple accident scenarios fed one lead system, with deeper qualification beyond raw CPL.

KLN Consulting

Consolidated by Service Line

Policy constraints favored broad delivery, but different financial intentions still required separation.

Operating Principle

Structure follows the business.

Budget, signal volume, buyer intent, sales cycle, platform restrictions, and lead economics determine the account architecture.
