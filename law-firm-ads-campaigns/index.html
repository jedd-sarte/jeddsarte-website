ViKing Law · Atty. Virginia KingPhiladelphia Metro / Bucks County, Pennsylvania · Personal Injury
Case Study 01 · Market & Acquisition Problem

Capture high-intent accident demand without letting generic PI traffic consume the budget.

ViKing Law's public positioning includes personal injury, car and truck accidents, and construction accidents. The paid-media approach treated each accident category as a different search market rather than one broad “personal injury lawyer” campaign.

Core Decisions
Separate motor vehicle and construction-intent traffic because search economics and case values differ.
Use exact and phrase match first in a high-CPC legal market.
Keep Meta broader, then let creative angles identify the accident scenario.
Optimize toward qualified intake and retained-case signals, not raw forms alone.

ViKing Law · Search Query Governance

Legal Search optimization happened at the query level.

Negative Keyword Themes
jobssalarylaw schoollegal aidcriminal defensetraffic ticketaccident reportpolice reportbody shopcar repairinsurance company jobsdefinition
Negative lists were applied by campaign theme, not blindly account-wide. A term irrelevant to car accidents can still be relevant to construction or workers' injury traffic.
Decision Rules
Search TermsReview frequently during launch. Add negatives aggressively when the query is research, defense-side, employment-seeking, reporting, or repair-related.
BudgetDo not equal-split by ad group. Weight spend toward case types producing stronger qualified-intake and downstream case signals.
CallsTrack calls separately from forms. A call is not a primary success event unless duration and intake disposition show actual legal-fit intent.
Offline SignalImport qualified intake and retained-client stages with click identifiers where available, instead of teaching Google that every form has equal value.

Peters Law Firm · Atty. Jordan T. GlaserCouncil Bluffs / Southwest Iowa · Iowa and Nebraska Experience
Case Study 02 · Market & Acquisition Problem

Multiple legal intents required separate economics, not one PI bucket.

Jordan Glaser's public practice includes personal injury, workers' compensation, wrongful death, and civil litigation. The media plan treated workers' comp differently from motor vehicle and catastrophic-injury demand because the searches, lead qualification, and follow-up path are different.

Core Decisions
Keep auto accident and workers' comp in separate campaigns.
Separate Iowa core geography from Nebraska expansion when intake coverage allows.
Build practice-specific landing pages instead of routing every legal query to the homepage.
Use case-result credibility in ads only where it supports the exact search intent.

Daryl Gray Injury Lawyers · Atty. Daryl GrayNew Orleans, Louisiana · Car, Truck and 18-Wheeler Accidents
Case Study 03 · Market & Acquisition Problem

In New Orleans PI, Google captured demand but could not be the only growth engine.

Personal injury Search is saturated and expensive. The strategy used Google for bottom-funnel demand capture while Meta generated incremental accident leads through specific pain points and collision scenarios.

Core Decisions
Keep Google tightly controlled around high-intent MVA queries.
Use Meta as the broader lead-generation engine rather than forcing Google to create demand.
Prioritize static creative diversity around accident and insurance pain points.
Judge both platforms on qualified intake, attorney review, and signed-case potential.

Daryl Gray · Lead Quality & Tracking

The conversion model moved from form submit to case progression.

Event 1
LeadForm or call enters CRM.
Event 2
Qualified IntakeAccident, injury, geography, and representation criteria verified.
Event 3
Attorney ReviewEnough facts for legal evaluation.
Event 4
Signed CaseClient retained when appropriate.
Event 5
Case ValueDownstream value can inform budget and bidding.
Technical Layer
Preserve GCLID / GBRAID / WBRAID for Google lead attribution.
Preserve Meta click and browser identifiers where available for CAPI matching.
Send qualified and signed stages back as offline conversions.
Track rejection reasons so media buying can identify bad traffic patterns.
A $150 lead that becomes an attorney-qualified case can be worth more than a $60 lead that is rejected immediately. The optimization target has to reflect that.

The Major Law Firm · Atty. Abasi MajorSan Antonio, Texas + Phoenix, Arizona · Personal Injury
Case Study 04 · Market & Acquisition Problem

Multi-market PI required market-level budget control.

The Major Law Firm publicly focuses on personal injury including car accidents, 18-wheeler and truck accidents, uninsured motorists, pedestrian accidents, wrongful death, rideshare, and serious injury. Texas and Arizona were treated as distinct media markets rather than one national campaign.

Core Decisions
Separate Texas and Arizona budgets so one market cannot consume the other's learning and spend.
Separate car accident from truck / 18-wheeler intent inside Search.
Use one broad Meta ad set per market with diversified accident creatives.
Keep conversion tracking granular enough to compare qualified lead economics by state and case type.
